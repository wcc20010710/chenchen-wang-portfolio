01 — ABOUT HUMAN FIRST / SYSTEMS SECOND

I design the invisible systems that make life feel effortless.

I’m Chenchen Wang, a creative technologist working where design, code and the built environment meet. For eight years I’ve helped ambitious teams turn complex systems — cities, homes, energy, data — into experiences that feel natural to the people inside them.

My work moves from research and product strategy to interface design and the final line of production code. I believe the best technology is felt before it’s understood.
